How to Play

Choose your weapon — Two players each command a set of dice as units in battle. Small dice move faster and have multiple weak attacks, big dice move slower but swing big. The d20 sitting on your throne is your life total. When it hits 0, you are defeated.

Goal

Get your opponent's d20 to 0 before they get yours. If you can get a piece next to it, you can attack it directly at any time.

HP & damage

Each die's starting HP equals its own max face value (a d12 starts at 12 HP, a d4 at 4, and so on). Damage persists between turns — it doesn't reset. When a piece hits 0 HP, it is destroyed.

Taking a turn

Choose 1 die, then move and/or attack with it. Once a piece attacks, it can't move again that turn.

The dice

  • d4 / d6 — fast, hit multiple times a turn, but each is locked to one movement axis (d4 diagonal-only, d6 ortho-only).
  • d8 / d10 / d12 — move in many directions but progressively slower, with progressively bigger hits.
  • d20 (throne piece) — moves like a d12, one square any direction, but can never attack.

Combat

Melee only — a piece must end its move adjacent to a target to attack it.
